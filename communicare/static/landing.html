Private, Safe, and Built for Real Families.

Picture communication cards help nonverbal individuals express what they need, understand daily routines, and connect with the people around them.

Making these boards by hand often takes 15 to 30 minutes every morning. CommuniCare creates personalized, ready-to-use boards in seconds while keeping your family records completely private.
