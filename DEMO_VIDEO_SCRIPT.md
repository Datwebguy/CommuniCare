## 🎙️ Scene-by-Scene Presentation Script

### [0:00 – 0:30] 🌟 Scene 1: Introduction & The Core Problem
**Visual On Screen**: Start on the CommuniCare Landing Page (`https://usecommunicare.vercel.app`). Scroll smoothly past the hero section and the 4 visual cards.

> **Voiceover (Warm, energetic, natural tone)**:  
> *"Hi everyone! My name is [Your Name], and I am excited to present **CommuniCare** — an autonomous assistive intelligence platform built with Google Gemini 3.5 Flash and Google Cloud for the Google Agentic Hackathon.*  
>  
> *Across the world, millions of nonverbal children, stroke recovery patients, and individuals on the autism spectrum rely on Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) picture cards to express their daily needs.*  
>  
> *But for family caregivers, special education teachers, and speech therapists, building these visual boards by hand is an exhausting daily chore. Finding the right symbols, resizing tiles, and color-coding grammar takes 15 to 30 minutes every single morning. CommuniCare completely eliminates that barrier."*

---

### [0:30 – 1:15] ⚡ Scene 2: Live Generation & Autonomous 5-Step Pipeline
**Visual On Screen**: Click **"Launch Studio →"** in the top navigation. The Caregiver Studio opens. Click on the preset chip **`🌅 Morning Routine`** or type the message into the textarea:  
`"Good morning Leo! Please take your medicine with a glass of water, and then we will have warm pancakes for breakfast."`

> **Voiceover**:  
> *"Let’s step into the Caregiver Studio. A caregiver can simply speak or type an everyday routine message. For example:*  
>  
> **'Good morning Leo! Please take your medicine with a glass of water, and then we will have warm pancakes for breakfast.'**  
>  
> *Now, when I click **Generate Cards**, our autonomous agent pipeline executes in under a second:*  
> 1. *It queries **Google Cloud Firestore** for Leo’s cognitive profile and visual contrast needs.*  
> 2. *It calls **Google Gemini 3.5 Flash** using the official **Google GenAI SDK** to extract core intents and simplify the sentence into clear plain language.*  
> 3. *It queries the open **ARASAAC catalog** for standardized pictograms.*  
> 4. *And it assembles the board according to the clinical **Fitzgerald Key** color standard — Yellow for People, Green for Actions, and Orange for Objects.*  
>  
> *Look at the result: A ready-to-use, color-coded visual communication board created instantly."*

### [1:15 – 1:45] 🔊 Scene 3: Voice Customizer & Inclusive Speech Synthesis
**Visual On Screen**: Click individual cards (`MEDICINE`, `WATER`, `PANCAKES`) to show the Midnight Wine outline and hear the audio. Then click **"Speak All"**. Next, click **"Voice Tone"** to open the voice settings modal and demonstrate persona switches.

> **Voiceover**:  
> *"Communication is two-way, so every card is interactive. Tapping a card vocalizes the word out loud with visual speaking rings.  
>  
> When I click **Speak All**, our asynchronous sequencer reads the entire plain language instruction from start to finish without clipping.  
>  
> Plus, every recipient has different sensory needs. In our **Voice Tone** studio, caregivers can switch between a cheerful **Child Friendly** voice, a grounded **Adult** tone, or a **Gentle & Calm** persona with adjusted pitch and pacing for sensory-sensitive individuals."*

---

### [1:45 – 2:20] 🧠 Scene 4: Multi-Turn Memory & Adaptive Learning
**Visual On Screen**: Click **"👍 Worked Well"** on the `MEDICINE` card. Then, in the message box, enter an afternoon message:  
`"Leo, remember to take your afternoon medicine before we go for a walk."` and click **Generate Cards**. Point mouse at the purple badge: **`✨ Personalized preference applied from memory`**.

> **Voiceover**:  
> *"What makes CommuniCare truly autonomous is its persistent multi-turn memory.  
>  
> When a caregiver clicks **Worked Well** on a card, CommuniCare updates Leo’s profile in Firestore, recording which symbols and vocabulary he recognizes best.  
>  
> Later in the day, when the caregiver enters an afternoon message — like remembering medicine before a walk — Gemini 3.5 Flash automatically cross-references his learned memory and prioritizes his preferred symbols, tagging the board with: **Personalized preference applied from memory**. It gets smarter and more tailored to each person over time."*

## 💡 Quick Tips for Recording

1. **Browser Setup**: Open `https://usecommunicare.vercel.app` in Chrome at 100% zoom.
2. **Audio**: Use a clear microphone and speak in a friendly, conversational pace.
3. **Cursor Movement**: Move your mouse deliberately and pause for half a second after clicking buttons so viewers can easily follow along.
4. **Pacing**: Don't rush through the generation step — let the judges see the 5-step pipeline illuminate!
